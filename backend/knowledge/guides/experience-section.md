# Structuring the Experience Section

List roles in reverse-chronological order with: title, company, location, dates. Under each role, bullets follow the Accomplished-X-by-doing-Y-measured-by-Z pattern.

## Prioritize outcomes over duties

A job description says what anyone in the seat would do; your resume should say what happened because it was you. Compare:

- Duty: "Responsible for maintaining CI pipelines"
- Outcome: "Rebuilt CI pipelines (GitHub Actions), cutting median build time 12min to 4min and flaky-test rate by 70%"

## Handling gaps and short stints

Never hide dates. Use a one-line note for meaningful gaps (relocation, education, caregiving) if asked; the resume itself should just present the roles confidently.
