# Tailoring a Resume to a Job Description

Generic resumes lose to tailored ones. Tailoring is 20 minutes of work per application:

1. Extract the JD's top 8-12 requirements (skills, domains, seniority signals).
2. Reorder your bullets so the most relevant evidence appears first in each role.
3. Mirror exact terminology for skills you genuinely have ("React" vs "front-end frameworks").
4. Rewrite the summary to lead with the overlap between you and the role.
5. Cut bullets that carry no signal for this particular role.

Never claim skills you lack — tailoring is emphasis and vocabulary, not fabrication.
