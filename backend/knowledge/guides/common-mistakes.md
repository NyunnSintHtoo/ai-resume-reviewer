# Common Resume Mistakes That Get Candidates Rejected

1. **Typos and inconsistent formatting** — read once aloud, once backwards, and have someone else read it.
2. **Weak phrasing** — "responsible for", "worked on", "helped with", "duties included".
3. **No numbers** — bullets that could describe anyone at any company.
4. **Buzzword self-praise** — "results-driven team player", "detail-oriented"; show, don't claim.
5. **Wall of text** — paragraphs instead of bullets; three-line bullets.
6. **Missing contact info or dead links** — every link must resolve.
7. **One resume for every job** — no tailoring to the target role.
8. **Unexplained tech soup** — listing 40 technologies with no evidence of use.
9. **First person and pronouns** — "I was tasked with..." reads amateur.
10. **Lying** — background checks and interviews surface it; instant rejection.
