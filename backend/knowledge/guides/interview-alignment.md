# Writing Bullets You Can Defend in Interviews

Every bullet is a potential interview question. Strong candidates write bullets that set up stories they want to tell (STAR: situation, task, action, result).

- If a bullet says "-40% latency", be ready to explain the baseline, the measurement, and your specific contribution.
- Prefer "Led the migration" only if you can name the decisions you made; otherwise "Migrated" or "Co-led".
- Team efforts: state your slice — "Built the ingestion service (1 of 4 engineers) for..."
- Keep a private "brag doc" with the full stories behind each bullet; refresh it quarterly.
