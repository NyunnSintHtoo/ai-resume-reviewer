# Resume Length and Density

- **Students / early career (0-5 yrs):** one page, no exceptions.
- **Senior (10+ yrs):** two pages maximum; the first page must stand alone.
- Cut anything older than ~10 years unless it is uniquely relevant.

## Density guidelines

- 3-6 bullets for recent roles, 1-3 for older ones.
- Bullets of one to two lines; a three-line bullet should be split or cut.
- Whitespace is a feature: cramped 9pt text reads as poor judgment.
- Target 400-800 words of substantive content for a one-page resume.
