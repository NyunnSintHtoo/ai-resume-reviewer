# Quantifying Impact on a Resume

Recruiters trust numbers more than adjectives. Aim for 50%+ of experience bullets carrying a metric.

## What to quantify

- **Scale:** users served, requests/day, dataset size, team size, budget
- **Improvement:** latency reduced, cost saved, conversion lifted, coverage increased
- **Frequency/time:** release cadence, time-to-resolution, hours saved per week

## Formula

Action verb + what you did + measurable outcome:

> "Optimized the ETL pipeline (Spark), cutting nightly batch time from 4h to 45min (-81%)."

## When you have no exact numbers

Use honest estimates and ranges ("~30%", "roughly 10K users"), counts of artifacts (endpoints, dashboards, services), or relative statements ("halved", "doubled"). Never fabricate metrics — interviewers probe them.
