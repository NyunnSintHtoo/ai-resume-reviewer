# Writing the Professional Summary

A 2-3 line summary at the top orients the reader in seconds. It should state role identity, years of experience, core specialty, and one differentiator.

> "Backend engineer with 4 years building payment infrastructure in Go and Python. Led the migration of a monolith to event-driven services handling 40M transactions/day. Interested in high-reliability distributed systems."

## Avoid

- Objective statements ("Seeking a challenging role...") - they describe what you want, not what you offer.
- Buzzword strings ("Results-driven team player with a passion for excellence").
- First-person pronouns; write in implied first person without "I".
