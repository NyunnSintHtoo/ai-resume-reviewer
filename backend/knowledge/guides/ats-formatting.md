# ATS-Friendly Formatting Rules

Most companies parse resumes with an Applicant Tracking System before a human reads them. A resume that parses badly is silently filtered out.

## Do

- Single-column layout, standard fonts, real text (not images of text)
- Standard section headings: Summary, Experience, Education, Skills, Projects
- Contact info (email, phone, city, links) as plain text near the top
- Dates in a consistent format (e.g. "Jan 2023 - Present")
- Save as PDF exported from a text editor, not scanned

## Avoid

- Tables, text boxes, multi-column layouts, headers/footers with critical info
- Graphics, icons, skill "rating bars", photos
- Unusual headings ("My Journey", "What I Bring")
- Keyword stuffing in white text — systems flag it and humans notice

## Keywords

Mirror the exact phrasing of the target job description for hard skills (e.g. "PostgreSQL" not just "databases") — ATS keyword matching is often literal.
