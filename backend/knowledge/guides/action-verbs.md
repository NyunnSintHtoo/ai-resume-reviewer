# Action Verbs That Strengthen Resume Bullets

Every bullet should open with a strong action verb in past tense (present for a current role). Weak openers like "Responsible for", "Worked on", or "Helped with" bury the candidate's agency.

## High-impact verbs by category

- **Leadership:** Led, Directed, Spearheaded, Drove, Coordinated, Mentored, Owned
- **Building:** Built, Developed, Designed, Architected, Engineered, Implemented, Shipped, Launched
- **Improvement:** Optimized, Reduced, Increased, Improved, Streamlined, Accelerated, Modernized, Refactored
- **Analysis:** Analyzed, Researched, Modeled, Forecasted, Evaluated, Quantified
- **Operations:** Automated, Migrated, Deployed, Scaled, Consolidated, Eliminated

## Rules

1. One verb per bullet; never stack ("Designed and developed and deployed").
2. Vary verbs across bullets — three consecutive "Developed" bullets read as filler.
3. Match the verb to the actual contribution: "Led" implies ownership of people or outcomes, not attendance at meetings.
