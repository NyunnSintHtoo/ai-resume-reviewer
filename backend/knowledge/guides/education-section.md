# Education Section Guidelines

- Recent grads: education near the top with GPA (if 3.5+), relevant coursework, honors.
- 3+ years of experience: education moves below experience; drop coursework and GPA.
- Format: degree, institution, graduation date (or expected date).
- Include bootcamps and significant certifications (AWS, GCP, CKA) — dated, and only if current.
- Never list high school once any post-secondary education exists.
