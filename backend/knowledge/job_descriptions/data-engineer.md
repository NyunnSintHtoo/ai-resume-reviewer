# Job Description: Data Engineer

**Company:** Halcyon Logistics
**Location:** Remote | **Level:** Mid-Senior (3-6 yrs)

## Responsibilities

- Build and operate batch and streaming pipelines (Airflow, Kafka, Spark)
- Model the analytics warehouse (BigQuery/Snowflake, dbt)
- Enforce data quality with tests, monitoring, and lineage
- Partner with analytics and ML teams on datasets and feature pipelines

## Requirements

- 3+ years of data engineering experience
- Expert SQL and strong Python
- Production experience with an orchestrator (Airflow, Dagster, Prefect)
- Cloud data stack experience (GCP or AWS), Docker, CI/CD

## Nice to have

- Kafka/PubSub streaming, Terraform, Iceberg/Delta, cost optimization at TB scale
