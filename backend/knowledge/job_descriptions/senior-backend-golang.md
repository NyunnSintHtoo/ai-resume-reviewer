# Job Description: Senior Backend Engineer (Go)

**Company:** Streamhaus
**Location:** Remote (global) | **Level:** Senior (5+ yrs)

## Responsibilities

- Design high-throughput services in Go handling 100K+ requests/sec
- Lead architecture for event-driven systems (Kafka, NATS)
- Set standards for reliability: SLOs, load testing, chaos experiments, incident review
- Mentor mid-level engineers; drive technical decisions across teams

## Requirements

- 5+ years backend experience, 2+ in Go at scale
- Deep knowledge of distributed systems: consensus, partitioning, backpressure, idempotency
- PostgreSQL and at least one KV/columnar store (Redis, Cassandra, DynamoDB)
- gRPC/protobuf, Kubernetes, observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry)

## Nice to have

- Rust, stream processing (Flink), multi-region architectures, open-source maintainership
