# Job Description: Data Scientist

**Company:** Meridian Commerce
**Location:** Remote (US/EU) | **Level:** Mid (2-5 yrs)

## Responsibilities

- Build forecasting and propensity models that drive merchandising decisions
- Design and analyze A/B tests; establish experimentation guardrails
- Own pipelines from raw events to features (SQL, dbt, Python)
- Communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders with clear visualizations

## Requirements

- 2+ years in data science or ML roles
- Strong Python (pandas, scikit-learn) and expert-level SQL
- Solid statistics: hypothesis testing, regression, causal inference basics
- Experience shipping models or analyses that changed a business decision

## Nice to have

- Airflow/Dagster, Spark, ML deployment (batch or real-time), LLM evaluation experience
